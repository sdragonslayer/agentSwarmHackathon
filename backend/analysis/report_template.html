How much repeated text inflates apparent spread

Text streams (agent memories, session goals, wiki page revisions) are rewritten again and again, and each version repeats most of the previous one. Counting every repeat as a new appearance overstates how often anything appears. This is occurrence inflation: a count of text occurrences, not an estimate of false transmission.
